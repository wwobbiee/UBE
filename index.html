    About UBE
    
        
    
          
    
            UBE by wobbie
            reads each chart into one shared beatmap model, then converts to your chosen game format. Everything is processed locally, in browser.
          
    
          
    
            Drop charts, folders or archives in Convert, choose the difficulties to include, and select an output format.
            Expand a difficulty to edit metadata or inspect its universal UBE JSON before downloading.
          
    
          
    
            Limits: 512 MiB expanded data per import, 10,000 files and five archive levels. BMS audio mixing is limited to
            ten minutes. Conversion messages identify omitted or altered data; failed source files are retained in a successful batch.
          
    
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Formats & conversion notes
    
        
    
          
    
            Archives: folders and nested ZIP containers are retained. Converted nested containers use .zip.
            One chart can download directly; archives, multiple charts or chart + assets download as ZIP. Extract the result
            into your game's song folder, or package a flat song as .osz / .qp for import.
          
    
          
    
            Timing: osu!mania and Quaver use millisecond note times. SM and BMS exports use a 1/48-beat grid
            and report quantization. Stops keep note times when exported to millisecond formats, but stop behavior and scroll
            effects can differ.
          
    
          
    
            Assets: common MP3/OGG/WAV and PNG/JPEG assets are copied. Unsupported media is omitted with a
            report; it is not transcoded. Missing references are reported. BMS samples are mixed into WAV for cross-format
            conversion when every required sample is supplied and decodable; keysounds then play automatically.
          
    
          
    
            Scope: DWI and PMS are import formats. Only mania osu! charts are supported. BMS RANDOM/conditional
            charts, LNTYPE 2, negative BPMs, warps and advanced timing extensions are rejected. Legacy SM lane layouts and BMS
            layouts may differ from the source game. This is not a visual note-placement editor.
